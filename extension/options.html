plico

Navigation for Helium, ready when you need it.

Navigation shortcuts reveal immediately. Ordinary shortcuts dismiss the navigator and continue to the page.

Shortcuts

Arrow keys also navigate. Add Shift to move tabs. Control+Tab cycles recent tabs; Command+1–9 and 0 select stacks. Escape cancels.

In a page editor, movement and Back keep their text-editing behavior until you explicitly open the navigator.

Settings stay in this browser profile. Plico never changes macOS shortcuts globally.
